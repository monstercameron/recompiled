The final reconciled candidate manifest contains **23 `target_type=Game` rows and 23 distinct game keys**. Of these, 17 keys are new to `games`; six were already in the live table. Four emulator projects are correctly marked `target_type=Software` and are excluded from game-link decisions. Their legacy `game` values identify supported systems/software, not games.

The importer-ready `wikipedia-links.json` contains **20 entries**: one direct English Wikipedia reference for each of the 17 new game keys, plus three upgrades from existing exact-title search fallbacks. All 23 actual game keys have a defensible direct article reference under the requested exact-identity rule. There are **no game-key holds and no game keys without a defensible reference**. No fuzzy matches were used. The audit records every exact key, candidate project title/URL, live database state, current URL/title, decision, direct article URL/title, evidence note, and deduplication note.
