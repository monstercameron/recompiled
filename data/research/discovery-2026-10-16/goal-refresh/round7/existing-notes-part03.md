# Existing game hydration, part 03

Verified on 2026-10-16. The profile manifest contains 11 exact `game_key` records and 52 individually sourced facts. The Wikipedia manifest contains one link record for each of the same 11 keys.

The Wikipedia links use direct game pages for **SD Gundam - Dimension War** and **Taekwon-Do**. **Progenitor** and **Terrors** link to English Wikipedia platform/publisher lists with a row for the exact title. The multi-title ScummVM group links to the ScummVM article. Exact-title search fallbacks are used for **PlayStation 1 games (multi-title)**, **RPG Maker 2000/2003 games (multi-title)**, **Sen no Hatou, Tsukisome no Kouki**, **Silent Hill: Chapter Two**, **Silmarils ALIS virtual-machine games (multi-title)**, and **Super Gomoku Narabe Renju**, for which no directly matching standalone English article was verified.

The multi-title entries are described as groups. The PlayStation group records its shared source platform and static-recompilation context. The RPG Maker group records the supported game-data formats and Windows PC origins. The ALIS group records the shared virtual-machine basis and notes that original platforms vary by title. The ScummVM group records its adventure-game scope and per-game original-platform variation. No release dates or publishers are assigned to the groups. “Silmarils” is recorded as the shared creator of the ALIS games, and no group is given one release date or a single genre where the titles vary.

For the single-title entries, **Progenitor** is matched to the 1994 PC-98 Koei game; MobyGames supplies its adventure/strategy and Rekoeition-series context, while the PC-98 release record gives the day-level release date. **SD Gundam - Dimension War** is the 1995 Virtual Boy tactical RPG, and the article notes that it was the last Virtual Boy game published in Japan. **Sen no Hatou, Tsukisome no Kouki** uses August’s official Windows product information for its genre, platform, and September 23, 2016 release, with GameFAQs corroborating the August developer and publisher credits. **Silent Hill: Chapter Two** is matched to the Java ME release called *Silent Hill Mobile 2*; its game-specific page supplies the developer, publisher, platform, genre, and September 2008 date. **Super Gomoku Narabe Renju** is the Naxat Soft Super Famicom board game released March 25, 1994. **Taekwon-Do** uses the English Wikipedia game article’s Japan-specific date and credits. **Terrors** is the Megas/Bandai WonderSwan horror visual novel released August 5, 1999; MobyGames describes its six branching stories.

All recorded date facts include `date_granularity`. Facts about a specific PC, mobile, or console release are limited to that release’s source; the profile does not transfer later ports or edition details onto the original release.
